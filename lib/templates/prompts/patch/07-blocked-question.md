# Blocked Question

Use `patch_status: blocked` when scope, repository, expected behavior, required access, or validation cannot be determined. Ask exactly one question that a human can answer in Jira.

Include:

- confirmed facts;
- what you inspected or attempted;
- the decision needed;
- the expected consequence after a reply.

Do not invent an answer, make a speculative change, or leave a vague “please clarify” message.
